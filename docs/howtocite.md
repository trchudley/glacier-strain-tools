# How to Cite

If you use this software in your work, please cite the origin as:

> Chudley, T. R., Stokes, C. R., Lea, J. M., Winterbottom, T., Law, R., Clason, C. C., Wytiahlowsky, H. E., & Dechow, J. L. (2026). Seasonal ice dynamics control the timing of crevasse drainage at a fast-flowing outlet glacier. _AGU Advances_. __7__(3), e2025AV002150. doi:[10.1029/2025AV002150](https://doi.org/10.1029/2025AV002150).

However, `strain_tools` at its core is a tool to faciliate implementation of approaches that have been developed by others. When describing the methods used, please additionally cite the following:

Process | Following...
--- | ---
Logarithmic strain rate | Nye _et al._ (1959); Alley _et al._ (2018)
Principal strain rate (analytical approach) | Nye (1959)
Longitudinal, transverse, and/or shear strain | Bindschadler _et al._ (1996)
Strain rate uncertainty | Poinar & Andrews (2021)
Deviatoric stress parameters | Cuffey & Paterson (2010)
